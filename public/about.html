Autorka vebsajta je profesorka engleskog, a u slobodno vreme se bavi veb developmentom, ili preciznije, i dalje uči veb development. Upravo to što i sama nešto uči smatra da joj pomaže da se smanji raskorak koji ponekad postoji između predavača i učenika. Neka pravila ili cele oblasti naučimo odmah a neka nam jednostavno ne idu. Za njih moramo da pronađemo pravi pristup. Otvorenost ka novom i istraživanju je najbolji put ka uspešnom savladavanju gradiva u bilo kojoj oblasti.
Hvala na izdvojenom vremenu,
Lana, Beograd, 30.07.2026.

The author of this website is a graduate English teacher (MA degree) who enjoys web development in her free time. More specifically, she is still learning web development. She believes that being a learner herself helps bridge the gap that sometimes exists between teachers and students. Some rules or entire topics are easy to grasp right away, while others aren't. For those, it is important to find the right approach. Being open to new ideas and willing to explore are the best path to successfully mastering any subject.
Thank you for your time,
